# Career-direction decision matrix

Read this reference when choosing among multiple plausible role families, revisiting positioning after market feedback, or deciding where the user should invest the next search cycle.

## Compare directions

For each role family, assess:

| Dimension | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Evidence fit | Cite completed work, scope, tools and outputs that support the direction. |
| Transferable capability | Mark as interpretation and tie it to evidence. |
| Critical gaps | Separate screening blockers from skills that can be learned after entry. |
| Transition cost | Estimate time and effort only when the assumptions are stated. |
| Resume explainability | Explain whether the move forms a credible narrative without inflating titles or ownership. |
| Work-style fit | Compare desired tasks, coordination load, travel, client exposure and other preferences. |
| Market evidence | Use current job samples or sourced research; otherwise mark `待验证`. |
| Option value | Explain likely adjacent paths, while labeling forecasts as judgment rather than fact. |
| Uncertainty | List missing evidence that could change the recommendation. |

Use `高/中/低` only when the basis is shown. Prefer `待验证` to an unsupported rating.

## Recommendation labels

- **主攻:** strongest evidence-to-opportunity path for the next search cycle.
- **试投验证:** plausible direction that needs a small market test before a larger investment.
- **备选:** credible adjacent path that preserves options or improves search volume.
- **暂缓:** material blocker, poor work-style fit or weak narrative that needs more evidence first.

Return one primary direction and at most one adjacent backup. Other plausible directions may be marked `试投验证` or `暂缓`; do not produce a long undifferentiated list.

## Decision rule

Do not average all dimensions mechanically. A hard constraint can rule out a direction. A lower current evidence fit may still justify `试投验证` when the gap is bridgeable and the direction strongly fits the user's desired work, but explain the bet and define what evidence would validate or invalidate it.
